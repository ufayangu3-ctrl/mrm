FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is mabati roofing?

Mabati is a common term used in Kenya for metal roofing sheets. This catalogue covers several supplied MRM roofing products.

What is Box Profile roofing?

Box Profile Mabati is one of the products listed in the supplied catalogue. The supplied variants include Gauge 28 and Gauge 30 entries with listed prices.

What does roofing gauge mean?

Gauge is a product specification used in the supplied catalogue. The catalogue lists different products at Gauge 28 and Gauge 30.

What is the difference between Gauge 28 and Gauge 30?

The supplied catalogue identifies them as different gauge specifications. For exact product characteristics beyond the supplied catalogue, confirm with the business.

How is the roof sheet quantity estimated?

The planning calculator uses area = length × width × pitch factor, then estimates sheets as ceiling(area ÷ (sheet length × effective cover width)).
