Kenya buying guide

How to compare mabati prices in Kenya

When comparing mabati prices in Kenya, check the gauge, sheet length, finish and exact product variant rather than comparing prices alone. The listed catalogue options on this site include Corrugated, Eurotile, Box Profile, Romantile and Versatile Mabati.

If you are specifically looking for a Box Profile mabati price in Kenya, review the individual colour and gauge listings because the supplied catalogue contains five variants with different listed prices. For Corrugated Mabati prices, the supplied listings include Gauge 28 and Gauge 30 at different prices.

Prices on this page are catalogue listings, not a guarantee of a current selling price. Confirm current price, availability, colour and purchasing arrangements before payment or collection.
